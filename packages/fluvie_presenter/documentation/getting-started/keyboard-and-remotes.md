# Keyboard and remotes

The presenter listens the moment it loads. No click-to-focus, on the web
too.

Moving forward: **Right**, **Down**, **Space**, **PageDown**, **Enter**, a
tap, or a swipe left. Moving back: **Left**, **Up**, **Shift+Space**,
**PageUp**, or a swipe right. **Home** and **End** jump to the first and
last slide. Type a slide number and press **Enter** to jump straight there.

Presenter remotes are just tiny keyboards. The common ones send PageUp and
PageDown for their arrows, F5 for "start", and a period for "blank" - all of
which do the right thing here. Pair the remote and present; there is nothing
to configure.

The chrome keys:

- **F** (or F5) toggles fullscreen, **Esc** leaves it.
- **B** or **.** blanks the screen black; **W** blanks it white. Any
  navigation or Esc brings the slides back exactly where they were.
- **O** opens the overview grid; pick a slide or Esc out.
- **S** opens the speaker window.
- **T** toggles the slide sidebar, **N** the notes panel, **H** the whole
  HUD. Prefer the mouse? The same actions sit as small buttons in the
  top-right corner of the stage.

Esc is polite: it closes the topmost thing first (a blank screen, a notice,
the overview) and only then leaves fullscreen.

The full table lives in the [shortcuts reference](../reference/shortcuts.md).
